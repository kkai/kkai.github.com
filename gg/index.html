DFG Großgeräte — Robotics / HCI / Lab-automation benchmark

Publicly funded Forschungsgroßgeräte (Art. 91b GG) instruments in robotics, human–machine interaction and lab automation, 2021–2026, harvested from GEPRIS. Use as a benchmark of what kind of instrument gets a positive DFG review.

ⓘ Full application texts (Anträge) are confidential and not public. This shows the public project record only: title, institution, subject area, programme, year, device group and abstract. The state-funded “Großgeräte der Länder” programme is not in GEPRIS (DFG only reviews it); “Forschungsgroßgeräte” is the closest public proxy and uses near-identical review criteria.
